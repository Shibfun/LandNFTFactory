Woofswap - Land NFT Factory Guide

Welcome to the Woofswap Land NFT Factory, a user-friendly interface for creating and minting Land NFTs on the Shibarium blockchain. This guide explains the key features and how to use the platform.

Overview

The Woofswap Land NFT Factory allows users to:

  • Create custom Land NFT contracts with specified name, symbol, max supply, and payment settings.
  • Mint NFTs based on unique (x, y) coordinates on a 1000x1000 grid.
  • View owned NFTs and created contracts.
  • Preview NFT metadata and images directly in the interface.

Requirements

  • MetaMask installed in your browser.
  • Wallet connected to the Shibarium network (Chain ID: 109).
  • Sufficient BONE or ERC20 tokens for contract creation and NFT minting (if applicable).

How to Use

1. Connect Wallet

  • Click the "Connect Wallet" button to link your MetaMask wallet.
  • Ensure the wallet is switched to the Shibarium network. The interface will prompt to add or switch if needed.

2. Create a Land NFT Contract

  • Enter the contract details:
    • Name: Alphanumeric, spaces, or hyphens (max 50 characters).
    • Symbol: Alphanumeric, spaces, or hyphens (max 10 characters).
    • Max Supply: Between 1 and 1,000,000.
    • Payment Type: Choose BONE (native) or ERC20 token.
    • Payment Token Address: Zero address (0x000...) for BONE, or a valid ERC20 token address.
    • Payment Amount: Amount required per NFT mint (in BONE or tokens).
  • Click "Create NFT Contract" and confirm the transaction in MetaMask.
  • Once confirmed, the new contract address will appear in the "Your Created NFT Contracts" section.

3. Mint a Land NFT

  • Enter the NFT contract address in the "NFT Contract Address" field to load contract details.
  • Specify the coordinates:
    • X Coordinate: 0 to 999.
    • Y Coordinate: 0 to 999.
  • Click "Mint NFT" and confirm the transaction. Ensure you have enough BONE or tokens for the minting fee (if set).
  • Minted NFTs will appear in the "Owned Land NFTs" section.

4. View NFTs and Contracts

  • Created Contracts: Lists up to 10 recently created contracts with their name, symbol, and address. Click "Copy" to copy the contract address.
  • Owned NFTs: Displays your NFTs with their coordinates and token IDs. Click "View" to preview the NFT image.
  • NFT Preview: Shows the SVG image of the selected NFT, including its name, symbol, and coordinates.

Notes

Network: The platform operates on Shibarium (Chain ID: 109). Ensure your wallet is connected to this network.

Gas Fees: Transactions require BONE for gas fees on Shibarium.

Input Validation: Coordinates must be unique and within 0-999. Contract names and symbols are case-insensitive and must be unique.

Contract Address: The factory contract is deployed at 0xeAf0155D4Fe75eF0cd357be8f63370Fb2506ceEF. Verify this address before interacting.
